RAG DEEP DIVE

MMR-> exact alog is very easy

first u choose the document say D1 which has highest similarity score

 now for D2 TO DN
selected={D1)
sim(q,D2)
argmax sim(D2,Dj) where j belongs to  documents in selected pool
MMR=lambda*(sim(q,D2)-(1-lambda)*(argmax sim(D2,Dj))
 same we do for D3 ,D4 ...

You first choose:

$$ D_1=\arg\max_D Sim(q,D) $$

because nothing has been selected yet.

Then for every remaining candidate, say \(D_2\):

1. Relevance to query
$$ Sim(q,D_2) $$
2. Redundancy with selected documents

For every \(D_j\) where \(D_j\in S\):

$$ Sim(D_2,D_j) $$

Take the maximum:

$$ \max_{D_j\in S} Sim(D_2,D_j) $$

This asks:

"How similar is D2 to its most similar already-selected document?"

3. Combine them
$$ \boxed{ MMR(D_2) = \lambda Sim(q,D_2) - (1-\lambda) \max_{D_j\in S}Sim(D_2,D_j) } $$

Then do the same for every candidate remaining in the candidate pool.

D2 → MMR = 0.42
D3 → MMR = 0.67
D4 → MMR = 0.31
D5 → MMR = 0.59

Choose:

$$ D_3=\arg\max MMR(D) $$

Now:

$$ S=\{D_1,D_3\} $$

Then you repeat the exact same process for the remaining candidates, but now redundancy is calculated against both \(D_1\) and \(D_3\).

So your mental algorithm is:

D1 = most relevant document
S = {D1}

while |S| < k:

    for each candidate D not in S:

        relevance  = sim(q, D)

        redundancy = max(
            sim(D, Dj)
            for Dj in S
        )

        MMR(D) =
            λ × relevance
            - (1-λ) × redundancy

    choose D with highest MMR
    add D to S

That's MMR. You've got it.(from chatgpt)

Faithfulness:
The Faithfulness metric measures how factually consistent a response is with the retrieved context. It ranges from 0 to 1, with higher scores indicating better consistency.

A response is considered faithful if all its claims can be supported by the retrieved context.

To calculate this: 1. Identify all the claims in the response. 2. Check each claim to see if it can be inferred from the retrieved context. 3. Compute the faithfulness score using the formula:

Answer Relevance->means how pertinent is the answer to the question itself
The evaluation metric, Answer Relevancy, focuses on assessing how pertinent the generated answer is to the given prompt. A lower score is assigned to answers that are incomplete or contain redundant information and higher scores indicate better relevancy. This metric is computed using the question, the context and the answer.

1. Context Relevance

Question → Retrieved Context

"Did the retriever retrieve useful information for this question?"

Example:

Question:

"What is Apple's revenue in 2025?"

Retrieved chunks:

Chunk 1: Apple's 2025 revenue      ✅
Chunk 2: Apple's iPhone sales       ✅
Chunk 3: History of Apple           ❌
Chunk 4: Apple's CEO                ❌


2. Faithfulness

Context → Answer

"Are the claims in the generated answer supported by the retrieved context?"

Context:

"Apple's 2025 revenue was $X billion."

Answer:

"Apple's 2025 revenue was $X billion and grew 15%."

If the context doesn't say anything about 15% growth:

Faithfulness ↓

This primarily evaluates your generator's grounding.
